A premier platform to learn, build, and explore the possibilities of quantum computing with IBM Qiskit.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about participation, registration, sessions, and venue.

Qiskit Fall Fest 2026 is a university-hosted quantum computing festival organized by Dr.RVR NRI Institute of Technology Deemed to be University in partnership with the global Qiskit community. It features a virtual inauguration, a 6-day online guest lecture series, an online Ideathon, and an in-person school Project Expo.

The event is open to undergraduate and postgraduate students, researchers, faculty members, school students (for Project Expo), software developers, and quantum enthusiasts from any academic institution or organization.

Yes! Participation in Qiskit Fall Fest 2026 is completely free of charge. Registration in advance is required.

No prior quantum computing experience is required. Sessions start from introductory fundamentals (such as Qiskit 101) to help beginners build understanding before exploring more advanced concepts.

Yes. All registered attendees who actively participate in the sessions, ideathon, or expo tracks will receive official certificates of participation co-branded by NRI Institute of Technology.

Qiskit Fall Fest 2026 is conducted in a Hybrid Mode featuring both virtual and in-person events:

• Online Events (Virtual Only): The Inauguration of Qiskit Fall Fest 2026 (23-11-2026), the 6-Day Guest Lecture Series (23–28 November 2026) featuring global researchers & experts, and the Ideathon (28 November 2026) are conducted virtually online.

• Offline Events (On-Campus): The Project Expo (30 November 2026, for in-school students) is held in-person on campus at Dr.RVR NRI Institute of Technology Deemed to be University.

The on-campus sessions will take place at the University Campus, Dr.RVR NRI Institute of Technology Deemed to be University, Agiripalli, Andhra Pradesh.
